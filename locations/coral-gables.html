FAQs

Questions from Coral Gables families

Do you help Coral Gables families who assume their student will attend the University of Miami?

We help families evaluate every option honestly, including UM. Fit and opportunity matter more than proximity or legacy alone, and we make sure students explore a genuinely balanced list.

Do you work with students at Coral Gables' independent schools?

Yes. We tailor our guidance to each school's specific grading system, course rigor, and culture, including schools like Ransom Everglades and Gulliver Prep.

Do you meet with Coral Gables families in person?

Yes, by appointment, and many families combine in-person sessions with virtual check-ins for flexibility.
