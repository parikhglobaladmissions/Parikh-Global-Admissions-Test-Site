FAQs

Questions from Saint Andrew's families

Are you affiliated with Saint Andrew's School?

No. Parikh Global Admissions is an independent college admissions counseling firm. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Saint Andrew's School — we work alongside families whose students attend the school, in addition to the school's own resources.

Do you work with both day and boarding students at Saint Andrew's?

Yes. We advise both day and boarding families, meeting in person by appointment in Boca Raton and virtually for boarding students and families based further away.

Do you coordinate with Saint Andrew's own college counseling office?

We encourage it. Our role is to complement the school's counseling team with additional one-on-one time, longer-range planning, and an outside perspective — not to replace the relationship families already have with the school.
