Pricing

Straightforward pricing for a long-term partnership

Admissions strategy is an investment, not a transaction. Here's what that investment looks like — no hidden fees, no guesswork.

How We Price Our Work

Most families work with us across multiple years, so our core pricing is package-based — built around the phase of the journey your student is in, not billed hour by hour. Each package includes a defined advising cadence (see the specifics on each tier below); additional sessions beyond that cadence are simply billed at our standard hourly rate. Every package is available in-person/hybrid across South Florida or fully virtual nationwide, and families who need lighter, focused support can also work with us hourly, or through a standalone test prep or essay program.

Questions

Common questions about pricing

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Multi-year and application-season packages can be split into monthly installments — we'll walk through the options during your consultation.

What's the difference between in-person/hybrid and virtual pricing?

Virtual sessions are priced lower to reflect reduced overhead, and are just as effective — many of our highest-scoring students work with us entirely online. In-person/hybrid pricing applies to families in our South Florida service area who want face-to-face sessions.

What if we need more sessions than our package includes?

Every package includes a defined advising cadence — see the specifics under each tier above. If your family needs more time in a given month, additional sessions are simply billed at our standard hourly rate ($195 in-person/hybrid, $165 virtual). No penalties, no renegotiating the package — just transparent add-on pricing.

What happens in the free consultation?

We talk through your student's goals, timeline, and current situation, then recommend the package that actually fits — never the largest one available.

What if my student is starting mid-year, or later than 8th grade?

Most families don't start on a clean grade boundary, and that's fine — pricing is prorated to the time remaining and the work involved, not a rigid calendar.

Do you guarantee acceptance results?

No — no ethical firm can. What you're paying for is a thoughtful, strategic process and honest guidance at every step, not a promised outcome.
