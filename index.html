The Admissions Timeline

Every grade has a job to do

A strong application isn't written senior year. It's built one grade at a time.

8th Lay the Foundation — 8th Grade Choose rigorous courses, explore interests broadly, and build strong study habits.
9th Establish Your Record — 9th Grade Commit to real academic rigor and grow one or two activities into leadership.
10th Build Momentum — 10th Grade Start the PSAT and a testing strategy; explore colleges on fit, not prestige.
11th Execute the Strategy — 11th Grade Sit for the SAT/ACT, narrow the college list, and begin essays and recommendations.
12th Finalize & Apply — 12th Grade Finalize the list, submit polished applications, and file for financial aid on time.
🎓 Decide & Transition — College Acceptance Compare offers, decide with confidence, and prepare for the transition.

FAQs

Common questions from families

When should families begin college planning?

Ideally, families begin planning by 8th or 9th grade, when academic choices, course rigor, and early extracurricular decisions start shaping a student's long-term profile. That said, we regularly work with families who begin later. Our role is to meet students where they are.

Do you guarantee college acceptances?

No. Ethical college advising does not include guarantees. What we do guarantee is a thoughtful, strategic process, honest and realistic guidance, strong execution, and applications that reflect the student authentically.

Do you work with families outside of South Florida?

Yes. Alongside our in-person locations across South Florida, we advise students and families virtually, nationwide and internationally.
