Services / Specialized Pathways

Tailored guidance for complex, competitive, and non-traditional paths

Some students follow well-defined admissions routes. Others pursue pathways that require earlier decisions, tighter coordination, and specialized knowledge.

What We Mean by "Specialized"

  • —Earlier timelines and eligibility requirements
  • —Additional application components
  • —Unique evaluation criteria
  • —Higher stakes for missteps or missed deadlines
  • —The need to balance multiple systems at once

Who Specialized Pathways Are For

  • —Students pursuing highly competitive or structured programs
  • —Students navigating athletic recruitment alongside academics
  • —Students with learning differences or executive functioning needs
  • —Students exploring early-assurance or alternative admissions routes

How the Process Works

The framework is the same across every pathway below — what changes is the timeline, the rules, and the details we help you navigate.

1

Assessment

Understanding your student's full picture — academics, goals, and the specific demands of their pathway.

2

Strategy & Roadmap

Building a coordinated plan that aligns your pathway's timeline with a strong core admissions strategy.

3

Ongoing Guidance

As things evolve — offers, evaluations, testing results — we help you adjust in real time.

4

Decision & Transition

Guiding your family through final decisions and a confident transition to college.

NCAA Athletic Recruiting

Navigating college admissions and athletic recruitment

For student-athletes, college admissions is not a single process — it is the intersection of academics, athletics, and NCAA rules, each operating on different timelines with different constraints. We help student-athletes and families navigate recruitment strategically and ethically.

Who This Is For

—High school student-athletes interested in competing at the college level

—Families navigating NCAA Division I, II, or III recruiting

—Students balancing rigorous academics with demanding training schedules

—Families unfamiliar with recruiting rules, timelines, or expectations

What Makes This Different

  • —NCAA eligibility requirements and academic benchmarks
  • —Recruiting calendars, contact periods, and sport-specific windows
  • —Differences between Division I, II, and III recruiting
  • —Official vs. unofficial visits, verbal offers, letters of intent

How We Support Your Family

  • —Building strong academic profiles that support eligibility
  • —Balanced school lists — recruited and non-recruited options
  • —Explaining recruiting language and interpreting coach communication
  • —Evaluating offers and interest realistically
What Families Gain Clarity on rules & timelines Fewer surprises Stronger positioning No overlooked options

Neurodiverse Student Support

Strength-based planning for students who learn differently

Neurodiverse students — including those with ADHD, learning differences, executive functioning challenges, anxiety, or other cognitive variations — bring unique strengths to admissions. We help families navigate this process thoughtfully, ethically, and strategically.

Who This Is For

—Students with ADHD, learning differences, or executive functioning challenges

—Students who require organizational, planning, or pacing support

—Families navigating accommodations, documentation, or advocacy questions

—Families who want to reduce stress while maintaining high expectations

What Makes This Different

  • —Differences between high school and college accommodations
  • —What colleges are — and are not — required to provide
  • —How and when to disclose learning differences
  • —Documentation requirements and timelines

How We Support Your Family

  • —Academic plans that reflect strengths and sustainability
  • —Choosing colleges aligned with learning needs and campus culture
  • —Helping parents shift from manager to coach over time
  • —Reducing anxiety through structure and realistic planning
What Families Gain Clear, compassionate guidance Reduced stress & uncertainty Stronger fit between needs and choice Supported, not defined by differences

International & Global Admissions

Strategic guidance across borders, systems, and expectations

International and globally mobile students face an admissions process more complex than most families realize — navigating different education systems, documentation requirements, visa considerations, and institutional expectations, often simultaneously.

Who This Is For

—International students applying to U.S. colleges or universities

—U.S.-based students living or studying abroad

—Students enrolled in international curricula (IB, IGCSE, A-Levels)

—Families navigating visa status, residency, or citizenship complexities

What Makes This Different

  • —How international transcripts are reviewed and contextualized
  • —Citizenship, residency, and visa classifications
  • —Testing timelines (SAT/ACT, TOEFL, IELTS, or equivalents)
  • —Financial aid and scholarship limitations for international students

How We Support Your Family

  • —Translating academic achievements across systems clearly
  • —Building school lists that reflect competitiveness and eligibility
  • —Developing narratives that reflect global perspective
  • —Coordinating clearly across time zones and school systems
What Families Gain Clarity on complex systems Stronger, more accurate applications Less last-minute stress Confidence across borders
